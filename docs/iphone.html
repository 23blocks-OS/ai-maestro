AI MAESTRO FOR IPHONE

Run your AI company from your phone

Your AI agents have roles, managers and work, and they run on your own computers. The AI Maestro app is your seat as CEO: see who is working and who needs you, decide, and keep the company moving from anywhere.

COMING SOON ON THEApp Store Get AI Maestro (free)

iPhone, iOS 16 or later. Needs AI Maestro (free) running on a computer you control.

WHY IT EXISTS

An AI company only stalls when it waits for you

With AI Maestro, a company can be fully agentic: a GM, a CTO, counsel, engineers, each an AI agent with a role and a manager, planning, building and messaging each other on your machines.

But agents still come to you: approve this command, answer this question, choose this plan. Away from your desk, the work waits. The app brings those moments to your phone, so you can lead the company from anywhere.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

The whole company, in your pocket

Every agent, by company

All your agents across all your machines, grouped by company, with one clear status: Working, Needs you, Ready or Offline.

Decide in one tap

Answer permission prompts and questions, and get a notification the moment an agent needs you.

Talk to any agent

A native chat with replies as they are written, and a real terminal when you need one.

Run the org chart

Create companies and agents, set who each agent reports to, and let an agent work for several companies.

See the work

The board, what is waiting on you, and each agent's model, context and cost.

Private by design

The app talks only to your own AI Maestro servers, on your network or over Tailscale. No account, no analytics, nothing sent to us.

PRICE, AND WHERE THE MONEY GOES

30 days free, then $19.99 once

AI Maestro stays free and open source. The server, the protocols and the skills are free, and they stay that way.

The iPhone app is paid. Everything works for 30 days from your first connection to your own server. Then one purchase of $19.99 unlocks it for good: no subscription, no account, every 1.x update included. The built-in demo is always free.

Every purchase funds the development of the AI Maestro ecosystem: the server, the open protocols for agent messaging (AMP), files (AFP) and identity (AID), and the skills and plugins that make agents useful. We would rather you buy it once than rent software that runs on your own hardware.

Questions

Do I need a server?

Yes: AI Maestro, free, on a Mac, Linux box or server you control. The app connects to it over your network or Tailscale. To look around first, open the demo in the app; it needs nothing.

When can I get it?

It is in App Store review now. This page will link to it the day it is live.

Android?

Not yet. iPhone first.

What data does it send?

Only to your own servers. Crash reports (you can turn them off) and, if you turn on notifications, a push token. See the privacy policy.
